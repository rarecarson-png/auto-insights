01 / Preparation overview

What changed, and why

Preserve a reliable source

The original data.csv remains unchanged. Assignment 2 describes the original data; this page documents its prepared successor. Differences between the two pages are expected.

Each retained record has a unique source_row_id. This points to its original data-record position, excluding the header, and is not a physical vehicle identifier.

Make limitations explicit

The cleaned master table keeps incomplete records. Missingness indicators and analysis-eligibility flags make exclusions visible. No values were filled with averages, modes, or guessed specifications.

Outlier flags identify records for review. A high price or unusual specification alone does not justify deleting a vehicle.

Row reconciliation: 11,914 original records − 715 exact duplicate copies = 11,199 prepared records. No additional rows were removed.

02 / All eight required cleaning tasks

Cleaning decisions

1. Missing values

Original blank cells stay missing. N/A in Market Category and UNKNOWN in Transmission Type are interpreted as unspecified information and converted to missing, with original labels preserved in the change log. N/A could mean not applicable or not assigned; it is not treated as a known market segment.

The first two numeric columns count only literal blanks. The last also includes unspecified labels and the one quarantined efficiency value. These counts therefore measure different stages of preparation.

2. Duplicate records

Exact duplicates were compared across all 16 source fields before cleaning. The first occurrence was retained and 715 later copies were removed. duplicates_removed.csv maps each removed source ID to the retained source ID. Make + Model + Year is not a unique key and was not used to deduplicate.

Identical published specifications might represent distinct omitted trims. Removing copies is an explicit analytical choice, so later findings should be checked against the original dataset when duplicate weighting matters.

3. Incorrect data types

All eight numerical fields use pandas nullable Int64, allowing whole numbers and missing values together. All nonblank numeric tokens parsed successfully. Model year stays an integer temporal attribute; it is not converted into a fabricated sale date.

CSV does not store data types. The downloadable dictionary records the intended schema; the reproduction instructions show how to restore it when reading the prepared file.

4. Inconsistent formats

All 16 source column names become snake_case. Surrounding whitespace is checked and trimmed if present; no surrounding whitespace was found in the inspected source strings. Standard categorical fields are lowercased, underscores become spaces, and repeated spaces are collapsed. Make and Model retain their original identity and spelling.

Examples: Engine HP → engine_hp, AUTOMATED_MANUAL → automated manual, and 4dr SUV → 4dr suv.

Its prepared highway value is missing until verified; no replacement such as 35 or 35.4 was guessed. The vehicle remains in the dataset.

Zero cylinders are retained because zero is not equivalent to a blank value. The 66 electric records are flagged for efficiency-unit review and excluded from the conservative fuel-economy subset, while their original efficiency values remain in the master table.

6. Outliers

Values strictly below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR are flagged using linearly interpolated quartiles on prepared, nonmissing values. These are statistical review flags, not confirmed errors. All flagged records remain in the master table.

748 retained records have MSRP equal to the observed $2,000 floor. They receive a separate review flag because price construction is not fully documented. No winsorization or automatic price removal was applied.

7. Inconsistent categorical values

Transmission, fuel type, drivetrain, size, and style receive consistent casing and spacing. Distinct AWD and four-wheel-drive labels stay separate. Make and model names are not merged using approximate matching.

Market Category is a multi-label field. Its comma-separated tags are trimmed, lowercased, sorted, and joined with a pipe: Luxury,Performance → luxury|performance. Token order no longer creates different representations of the same set. Missing category information stays missing. Explode these tokens only for a category-level analysis, where one vehicle may contribute to multiple categories.

8. Data transformations

log10_msrp = log10(msrp) supports later analysis of the skewed price distribution while retaining the original MSRP. Missingness flags, review flags, and five IQR outlier flags are added, along with source-row provenance and two analysis-eligibility flags.

11,130 core-analysis records

Nonmissing make, model, year, horsepower, and MSRP. The 69 rows with missing horsepower are retained in the master dataset but excluded from this specific subset.

11,129 fuel-economy records

Known non-electric fuel type, city efficiency, and highway efficiency. Excludes 66 electric records, three records with missing fuel type, and the one quarantined highway value.

These overlapping subsets answer different needs. Neither is a universal complete-case dataset. A model using additional variables must define and report its own exclusions. Future train/test splits must precede any learned imputation, scaling, or encoding to prevent leakage.

04 / Research connection

Data Preparation / Methodology

The original Kaggle Car Features and MSRP dataset contained 11,914 records and 16 fields. The raw CSV was preserved, and each prepared record received a source_row_id corresponding to its original one-based data-record position. Exact duplicates were identified across all 16 original fields before any transformations; 715 repeated copies were removed while retaining the first occurrence. This produced 11,199 records. This is a record-level deduplication decision, not proof that repeated rows represent the same physical vehicle.

Column names were standardized to snake_case. Numerical fields were converted to nullable integers, while text categories were standardized without merging distinct makes, models, or drivetrain categories. Comma-separated market-category tags were lowercased, sorted, deduplicated within each record, and separated by pipes. Blank cells remained missing. The source labels N/A in Market Category and UNKNOWN in Transmission Type were treated as unspecified information and converted to missing values. No numerical or categorical values were imputed. This choice avoids inventing specifications, but available-case analyses may still be biased if missingness is systematic.

Positive-value and nonnegative-value checks were applied according to each field's meaning, and model years were screened against a broad 1886–2027 range fixed for this project. All nonblank numeric source tokens parsed successfully, and none failed those basic range checks. One non-electric vehicle's highway fuel-economy value of 354 exceeded the separate review threshold of 150 and was set to missing pending source verification. Its original value and source-row identifier were preserved in the change log. This is a conservative analysis rule rather than a verified correction. Electric-vehicle efficiency values were retained and flagged because their unit comparability requires verification.

Potential outliers in MSRP, horsepower, cylinders, and city and highway fuel economy were flagged using values strictly outside Q1 − 1.5 × IQR and Q3 + 1.5 × IQR, with linearly interpolated quartiles calculated after preparation on each field's nonmissing records. Flagged records were retained. Prices equal to the observed $2,000 source minimum were also flagged for review. A base-10 logarithm of positive MSRP was added alongside original prices to support analysis of the skewed price distribution. No winsorization, inflation adjustment, or causal interpretation was applied.

The prepared master table contains 35 columns: 16 renamed source fields and 19 provenance, transformation, missingness, review, and eligibility fields. There are 11,130 records with nonmissing make, model, year, horsepower, and MSRP for the defined core analysis, and 11,129 records in a conservative fuel-economy subset requiring a known non-electric fuel type and both efficiency values. These subsets overlap and are not universal complete-case samples. Future analyses must report their own inclusion rules. Validation confirmed row-count reconciliation, unique source-row identifiers, no duplicate prepared source-field combinations, nullable integer types, and a reversible logarithmic transformation. All cell edits and removed duplicate identifiers were exported for audit and reproducibility.

06 / Source & limits

What this preparation does not resolve

Dataset: Car Features and MSRP, CooperUnion on Kaggle. Source description and initial investigation: Assignment 2. Prepared source: repository data.csv.

These are vehicle specification records, not individual sales or a representative sample of current buyers. MSRP is treated as the source's dollar-denominated price field; it is not a verified current transaction price. Model years span 1990–2017, and prices were not inflation-adjusted. Popularity has no established interpretation in this project and should be excluded from explanatory claims until its definition is verified.

Missingness, undocumented price construction, omitted trim identifiers, and mixed efficiency-unit conventions remain research limitations. Cleaning improves consistency and traceability; it does not remove these limitations.
