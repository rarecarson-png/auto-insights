An interactive companion to the Car Features and MSRP research project. Filter vehicles by make, size, transmission and model year; hover for details, zoom or box-select points, and use the chart toolbar to explore. Charts update together when filters change.

Interaction and methodology

Interactions: hover tooltips, make/size/transmission dropdowns, model-year range filtering, price thresholds, zoom, box selection and Plotly pan/reset. The three charts and summary cards are linked through shared filters. Calculations: medians use nonmissing numeric records; rows with missing values are omitted only from charts requiring those fields. MSRP is manufacturer's suggested retail price, not transaction or used-car resale price.

Source: CooperUnion, Car Features and MSRP (Kaggle). This site does not claim predictive-model evaluation results.
