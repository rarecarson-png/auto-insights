INITIAL DATA INSPECTION

Data quality & limitations

Missing and unspecified values

  • Engine HP: 69 blank values
  • Engine Cylinders: 30 blank values
  • Number of Doors: 6 blank values
  • Engine Fuel Type: 3 blank values
  • Market Category: 3,742 entries labeled N/A
  • Transmission Type: 19 entries labeled UNKNOWN

N/A and UNKNOWN are retained as source labels. Their treatment will be documented during data preparation.

Repeated records

Comparing all 16 columns identifies 715 repeated rows beyond the first occurrence of each exact row.

The original file and explorer preserve these records. Identical recorded specifications do not necessarily prove a collection error. We will investigate before deciding how to handle them.

Price interpretation

MSRP means manufacturer suggested retail price. The price field requires further validation, including unusually low values and differences across model years.

The data does not establish current sale prices, resale values, ownership costs, or inflation-adjusted prices.

Scope and representativeness

The file covers model years 1990–2017. It is not a current vehicle market inventory or a random sample of consumer purchases.

Brand comparisons describe the records included here. They should not be interpreted as sales-weighted market averages or evidence of causation.
