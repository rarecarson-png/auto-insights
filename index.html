ASSIGNMENT 01

Research question & proposal

Proposal draft

Research topic

The relationship between vehicle characteristics and manufacturer suggested retail price, or MSRP.

The real-world problem

Car buyers compare vehicles with different brands, engine specifications, body styles, and fuel economy ratings. These differences make it difficult to understand how listed prices relate to measurable features.

Why this matters

Understanding price patterns could help consumers compare vehicles and help marketers explain how products are positioned across different market segments.

This project examines the field labeled MSRP in the selected dataset. It does not estimate current dealership prices, used-car resale values, or the total cost of ownership. Price definitions and unusual values require further source validation.

PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTION

How do vehicle specifications and brand relate to MSRP in the Car Features and MSRP dataset?

Research sub-questions

  1. How does MSRP vary across vehicle brands?
  2. What relationship exists between horsepower and MSRP?
  3. How does MSRP differ across body styles and vehicle sizes?
  4. How do city and highway fuel economy relate to MSRP?
  5. Does the relationship between horsepower and MSRP remain after accounting for brand and model year?

Preliminary hypotheses

H1: Vehicles with higher horsepower will tend to have higher MSRP.

H2: MSRP will differ across brands, even after accounting for horsepower and model year.

These are predictions to test, not established findings. Observed associations will not establish causation.

THE DATA FOUNDATION

Meet the dataset

Car Features and MSRP

The selected source is Cooper Union's dataset on Kaggle. The uploaded CSV contains 11,914 records, 16 columns, and 48 brands, covering model years 1990–2017.

Explore the original records on the dedicated dataset page. Search vehicle names, filter by brand, sort prices, and review the data dictionary and initial quality checks.

The explorer preserves the original records, including missing values and repeated rows. Cleaning decisions will be documented separately.
